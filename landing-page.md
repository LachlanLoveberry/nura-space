## Frequently asked questions

### What is Nura Space?

Nura Space is an Australian workplace management platform designed to help organisations manage hybrid work through intelligent booking systems, workplace analytics, occupancy tracking, and space optimisation tools. Founded in Melbourne in 2017, Nura Space provides enterprise-grade desk booking, meeting room booking, locker booking, car park booking, visitor management, block planning, and IoT-enabled workplace intelligence solutions.

### What does Nura Space do?

Nura Space helps businesses coordinate how employees use office space. Employees can book desks and meeting rooms, while workplace leaders can track utilisation, monitor trends, and make data-driven decisions about their physical workplace.

### Does Nura Space support hybrid work?

Yes. Nura Space is designed specifically for hybrid workplace management. It helps organisations manage flexible attendance, coordinate team schedules, and optimise office occupancy across changing work patterns.

### Is Nura Space only for desk booking?

No. While desk booking is at the core, Nura Space is a full workplace management platform, covering meeting room booking, locker booking, car park booking, visitor management, team collaboration, workplace analytics, employee wellbeing, and workplace safety.
